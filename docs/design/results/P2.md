## Decision (Gate 1, 2026-10-29)
**Go with variant B:** mess dims the sunlight and slows the work pace, and the people's mood shows it (user: "sunlight +
mood and workflow slow down"). Care is a pillar. Recorded in [decisions.md](../../decisions.md).

## Agent playtest (tester, real touch via InputTestFixture)
- Run 1 passed the build list: wipe/flick by touch in both variants (B: 7/7 flicks), correct welcome-back after +8 h
  and +48 h, penalty only in B (−24 % → −3 % → "Work is going well"), 0 console errors, readable at 1080x1920,
  1080x2400 and 1640x2360. Shared tests 11/11.
- Fixed from run 1:
  - [real] B's real drop was about −36 % and growth was half of A's, because mess also lowered mood.
    Mood now ignores mess in both variants. Same seed, +48 h: B 35 people vs A 32.
  - [real] A grumble fired on the welcome-back frame. Grumbles now wait 12 s after a return.
  - [real, computed] Under a notch, B's two-line ledger overlapped the banner. The banner now sits
    `bannerBelowSafe` = 96 below the safe top.
  - [polish] The banner now grows with its text.
- Polish for the user to decide:
  - A normal-speed wipe also flicks junk, so flicking is never learned separately.
  - Glass grime is only a third of the mess level: smudged glass can coexist with "−3 %" and no grumbles.
  - Lab and bottom buttons are under 44 pt.
  - "Is anyone going to clean up?" and the orange penalty line lean naggy.

- Tester run 2: B–F passed (12 s grumble quiet, banner clears the ledger, the hold switches by real touch and ignores taps, short holds and drags). It failed A: the seed made lab B ~25 % smaller by luck. Fixed with the split random stream above.
- Then, from the user's review of the polish list: the start-decides gesture, glass 50 % of mess, 44 pt buttons (88 canvas units: Step away, Feedback, Done), and the reframed ledger and lines.

- Then, from the user's pick of the run-3 polish:
  - A cancelled grab (second finger) is silent.
  - A grabbed piece lifts and tilts, and snaps back when dropped.
  - A press with no travel says "Flick it out".
  - Junk ×2.4 and weeds ×1.8 bigger.
  - Feedback, Done and the lab row sit above the home indicator.
  - Quick self-check: compile clean, tests 13/13, screenshots.
