# P8 The living land: build notes (2026-11-02)

Prototype P8 puts the Ecology Lab's model (#228, `docs/design/spikes/ecology-lab.md`) into the real game, following
[keepers-hands.md](../keepers-hands.md) §2, §2.1 and §5. It is **off by default** (`SimConfig.ecology = false`); with it
off, the slice plays as before. Turn it on with the debug panel's **Living land (P8) → Barren box**: that starts a new
box barren and keeps the switch across restarts (`p8.ecology` in PlayerPrefs). **Old box** turns it off again.

Issues: #243 (sim), #244 (the land greens), #245 (water and air), #246 (the tribe), #247 (sound), #248 (first run,
debug, smoke). The kit button, #249, waits for the user's sign-off.

## What's in

- **Sim (#243).**
  - `Sim/Eco/` carries the lab's model: one step a season, its own dice.
  - The camp gate (meadow ≥ 15 %, rabbits ≥ 2): before it, nothing new is built (one tent and a fire). The village gate
    (a pond ≥ 20 cells, ≥ 8 grown trees) joins `villageAt`.
  - Nothing is built below the flood line. The pond comes from the water table. The forest and meadow flags follow the
    cover. Saves and catch-up are covered.
  - The game sim reproduces the lab exactly on seed 7 (with care: camp Y4.3, tree grown Y13.5, water Y20, village Y25.3).
- **The land (#244).**
  - Bare ground is dry, cracked dirt that darkens where it's wet. Green spreads cell by cell (blended between cells).
  - Tufts, tall grass with flowers, bushes, seedlings, young and grown trees and reeds stand where the cover says.
  - The Keeper's tree has its own look per stage (a mound, a sprout, a sapling, young, grown and bigger than the forest)
    and shows thirst (fallen leaves) or weeds.
  - Only the quarters that changed rebuild.
- **Misting (#244), a temporary input until #249.**
  - Hold a finger still on the land for 0.3 s: a fine mist drifts down, the camera holds still, and sliding mists along.
  - The sim greens the core at once and a green patch spreads out on the spot.
  - The people nearby react with the rain's reaction: hands out and faces up, the kids splash.
  - A drag still orbits and never mists.
- **Water and air (#245).**
  - The pond is a sheet at the sim's water level over the hollow, with the ground hiding its edge, plus reeds.
  - The water table is a wet blue band in the soil cut with a bright line at the water, rising as the mists soak down.
  - The glass shows the air's humidity as fog climbing from the soil line, with beads of water in it. The cloth wipes
    them off.
- **The tribe (#246).**
  - Before the camp gate everyone who works digs roots in the dry hollow and carries them to the fire; the toolmaker
    knaps by the fire.
  - No hunting, chopping or evening fishing on a dead land. Fishers dig while the hollow is dry.
  - The chronicle notes the signs: the first green, the first rabbit, the first bird, the water, the gates.
- **Sound (#247).**
  - A barren bed (dry wind, sand hiss, crackling dirt, a loose stone) follows the bare share of the land. It fades out
    below 15 % bare and never comes back.
  - Crickets come with the first green (a faint chirr by day until the birds come), birds with the first bird, frogs
    with the water.
  - Both loops are made in code (ours, CC0) for the gray-box.
- **First run (#248).**
  - On a barren box: wipe the misted glass → no weed patch → "Hold to mist" by the camp → the ground greens and the line
    "Green, where the mist fell. They come to stand in it." → name it → done.
  - No year skip, no rain tray, no rain wish.
- **Debug panel:** a "Living land (P8)" section with:
  - a barren or old box;
  - mist (random, or at the camp);
  - +1 season, +1 year, +5 years;
  - the camp and village gates;
  - water up, dry or humid air;
  - plant the tree, and tree stages 0–4.

  The ambience layers' on/off toggles cover the barren bed and the frogs.

## Open for the user

1. **The look** (gray-box): the cracked dirt, the green spreading, the pond, the water-table band in the soil cut (it
   shows a wide wet band from the start, because the table sits under the whole box), and the glass fog and droplets.
2. **The sounds**: the barren bed and the frogs are synthesized placeholders. Do they read? Replace them with recorded
   CC0 ones?
3. **The mist's answer at phone zoom**: the greened patch is small at the default framing. Should it be bigger (a
   larger mist radius), or should the camera lean in on the first mist?
4. **Hold to mist** is a stand-in for the kit button (#249). Keep the hold gesture as a shortcut once the kit exists?
5. Still open from the lab write-up (§6): the tree's light need is a weak lever; the people water the tree after 3
   mistings; the forest's edge needs a brake (woodcutters or the snips).
6. **Not built yet**:
   - the fire moving next to the first green;
   - rabbit and bird dolls, frogs and ducks (the signs are chronicle lines and sounds for now);
   - the Keeper's tree's seasonal looks (bare in winter, colour in autumn);
   - the time-lapse showing the land's history (it shows the old land).
