# P4 Book of Ages: build status (2026-10-29)

Tests H4 ("being able to browse the civ's history is a reason to linger and share"). Spec:
[prototype-plan.md](../prototype-plan.md) §5. Scene `Assets/Prototypes/P4_BookOfAges/P4_BookOfAges.unity` (menu:
AquariumCiv → Agent → Prototypes → Build P4 Book of Ages), opened from the Test Hub. Results go here after testing.

## Build plan
- **Hypothesis:** browsing the village's history makes you linger and want to show it to someone.
- **The one variable:** the history view, opened from the same "History" button.
  - **A · Time-lapse:** a timeline at the bottom; drag it and the village in the glass rewinds and regrows (buildings
    pop in, the crowd and the named people of that year appear). Story pins on the timeline; the story nearest the
    handle shows above it with a portrait. Play replays the century in about 15 s.
  - **B · Illustrated book:** paper pages: a cover with the five founders, one chapter per 25 years (its top stories with
    portraits, population and buildings at start and end), a Legends gallery of everyone who ever lived (the dead
    faded), family trees. Swipe or arrows to turn.
- **Same in both:** P3's sim and stories, 100 game years of pre-generated history (same seed for both), notables in the
  world with person cards, a Snapshot button, plain returns (P3's B line), the wipe gesture.
- **In scope:** yearly snapshots recorded by the sim (`YearSnapshot`: year, population, buildings, notables, mood; the
  buildings keep their founding month, so replay is a filter), both views, snapshot.
- **Out of scope:** sharing to Photos (needs a native plugin; the snapshot is saved in the app's folder and logged), real
  video, eras.
- **Simplest readings:** "event pins you can tap" = pins on the timeline; letting go near one snaps to it and shows its
  story. "Notable portraits" = P3's portraits. P4 has an optional 3-day diary (same scene, real time) for the "opens
  history unprompted" metric; the lab is the main test.
- **Metrics:** `history` open (`how`, `since_hint_s`, mode), close (`dwell_s`, `scrub_years` or `page_turns`), `pin`,
  `page` (from, to, dwell, swipe or button), `play`, `snapshot`; counters `history_opens`, `history_s`, `scrub_years`,
  `page_turns`, `snapshots`. Survey Q4 "I'd show this to a friend".
- **Lab pacing (~7 min per variant):** a fresh 100-year village → History → browse (the guide gives three small finds:
  the year the first windmill went up, the oldest person who ever lived, a snapshot you'd send) → close → Done.
- **Debug panel (P4 row):** Open history, Open the other view, Story now, +1/+10/+50 years, Fresh lab world.
