# P3 While You Were Away: build status (2026-10-29)

Tests H3 ("a curated chronicle with named notables makes the return the highlight, and players remember their civ's
story"). Spec: [prototype-plan.md](../prototype-plan.md) §5. Scene `Assets/Prototypes/P3_WhileYouWereAway/P3_WhileYouWereAway.unity`
(menu: AquariumCiv → Agent → Prototypes → Build P3 While You Were Away), opened from the Test Hub. Results (metrics, diary,
survey quotes, decision) go here after testing.

## Build plan
- **Hypothesis:** story cards about named people make coming back the highlight, and people remember what happened.
- **The one variable:** how the return is presented.
  - **A · Chronicle cards:** 3–7 cards, top story first (salience, +0.25 for the pinned person), each with the person's
    portrait (the same KayKit character that walks the village), "Show me" flies the camera there.
  - **B · Plain return:** the changed village plus one stats line ("You were away 8 hours: 8 years passed. +4 people, 1 new hut.").
- **Same in both:** the story generator, notables walking the village as themselves (bigger, with a marker), tap a person
  → their card (portrait, trait, age, family, friends, last 3 stories), pin a favourite (gold marker with their name, their
  stories rank first in A), the welcome wave, the wipe gesture.
- **In scope:** ~30 event templates (weddings, friendships, feuds and make-ups, apprentices, the eldest, 14 trait
  incidents with 2 lines each, lost-and-found, storms, rainbows, fog, droughts, bumper harvests, first snow, ducks, a
  sheep in the tavern, naming the settlement, anniversaries) on top of the core's births, deaths, firsts, milestones,
  festivals and lean winters; lineages (parent, partner, children); pins.
- **Out of scope:** the Book of Ages (P4), nudges (P5), eras (P6). Care has no consequences (no output drop, no mood link):
  that's Gate 1's question and would confound this A/B.
- **Simplest readings of vague spec:** "notable portraits" = a render of the notable's KayKit model on a disc tinted by
  family (5 models, so faces repeat; the tint and name tell them apart). "Fly-to camera" = the existing orbit camera
  re-pivots and zooms to the person or building, offset so it lands above the card. Stories draw from their own random
  stream, so they never change the economy (or P2).
- **Metrics logged:** `chronicle_open` (cards shown, stories available), `chronicle_view` per card (id, kind, rank, dwell,
  fly-to, how it was left), counters `cards_read`, `card_dwell_s`, `fly_to`, `notable_cards`; `notable` tap / pin / unpin /
  card dwell; `return` now includes `stories`; survey Q3 (recall, the key measure) and Q4 "I care about specific people in
  my tank". Session length as always.
- **Lab pacing (~7 min per variant):** each variant starts from the same fresh world (seed 1234, 20 years of
  pre-generated history, clean glass). Step away +8 h → read the return → tap people, pin one → step away +24 h → return
  again → Done for now. Diary (4 days): real time, variant alternates by day.
- **Debug panel (P3 row):** Story now, Notable dies, Notable born, Cards: last 10 y, Pin someone, Unpin, +1/+10/+50
  years (quiet), Fresh lab world.

## Built
| Piece | Notes |
|---|---|
| Story layer (shared) | `SimCore.Story.cs`, on only when `SimConfig.storyEvents` is set. Own random stream (`storyRng`), so turning it on never shifts births or deaths. Anti-repeat: a person tells each kind of trait story at most once in 15 years; weather and oddities have their own gaps. Entries can link a second person and a building. |
| Notables in the world (shared) | `NotableView`: one character per living notable aged 6+, model by id, 1.15× the crowd, a teal dot overhead (gold + name when pinned). The crowd shows the rest of the population. |
| Portraits (shared) | `PortraitStudio`: an off-screen camera renders each KayKit model once into a 256² texture. |
| Person card (shared) | `NotableCardPanel`: tap a notable → card, Pin / Unpin / Close. |
| Cards (P3) | `ChronicleCards`: bottom panel above Feedback / Done, page dots, Skip all / Show me / Next (Done on the last). |
| Lab | Shared `LabHud` (ledger with year, people, settlement name; Step away +1/+8/+24/+48 h; hold the ledger 2 s to switch diary/lab) and `LabStrip` (Test Hub section below). |
| Tests | `StoryTests`: P2's world fingerprint is unchanged (both variants), stories never shift the economy dice, catch-up equals live with stories, 8 h after a 20-year prehistory gives 3+ stories in ≥ 24/30 seeds, stories link real people and marriages are mutual, old saves load. Shared assembly 21/21. |
