# P5 Keeper's Nudge: build status (2026-10-29)

Tests H5 ("indirect nudges make players feel they shaped the history, without micromanagement or helplessness").
Spec: [prototype-plan.md](../prototype-plan.md) §5. Scene `Assets/Prototypes/P5_KeepersNudge/P5_KeepersNudge.unity`
(menu: AquariumCiv → Agent → Prototypes → Build P5 Keeper's Nudge), opened from the Test Hub. Results go here after testing.

## Build plan
- **Hypothesis:** gentle nudges make you feel you shaped what happened, without micromanaging.
- **The one variable:** the form of the nudge (same three leans, same timing, same credit).
  - **A · Omens:** a harvest totem (lean: food), a craft totem (lean: making), a faith shrine (lean: festivals, faith).
  - **B · Shape the land:** rain on a spot (food), plant a grove on an empty tile (making), dig a channel from the pond
    on an empty tile (food and a mill).
- **How a nudge works (shared sim, `SimCore.Nudges.cs`):** for 3 game years the village leans (food ×1.35, or wood
  ×1.5, or mood up and extra festivals). After at least 8 game months the people answer with a building beside the
  nudge (a field, a mill, a market, a chapel...), and the chronicle credits the Keeper: "After the Keeper's rain, Ada
  Lund planted a new field." Each nudge can be used again after 3 game years. People walk over to look at a new nudge.
- **In scope:** 3 nudges per variant, placement by tap, the visible answer within ~1–3 game years, chronicle credit
  (a banner live, and in the welcome-back line), look-time on nudges (`nudge` log category).
- **Out of scope:** terraforming beyond these three, eras, the Book of Ages.
- **Simplest readings:** "~1–3 game years, faster in lab" = the lab runs at ×60 (a real minute is a game year), so an
  answer comes 1–3 minutes after the nudge. "Credited in the chronicle" = a `keeper` chronicle entry, shown as a banner
  when it happens and on return. Survey Q4 "I felt I shaped what happened", plus a sixth free-text question: "Did you
  have too much or too little control over what happened? Why?"
- **Metrics:** `nudge` (tray, choose, place with kind, slot, year; cancel), `nudge_effect` (kind, game months and real
  seconds to the answer, building), `checkin` (nudges in the last check-in) for "a nudge is used in ≥ 60 % of
  check-ins", counter `nudges`, look-time on the `nudge` category.
- **Lab pacing (~7 min per variant):** fresh world → Nudge → place one → watch the people gather and answer (1–3 min)
  → place another → step away 8 h → read the welcome line → Done. Diary (3 days): real time (an answer takes 1–3 real
  hours), the variant alternates by day.
- **Debug panel (P5 row):** place each nudge kind, Answer now (+3 years quiet), Reset cooldowns, time jumps, Fresh lab world.
