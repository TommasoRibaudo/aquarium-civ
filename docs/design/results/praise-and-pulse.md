# What players love, and what the quick questions say

A running record of the praise notes (`fb:praise`) and the quick-question answers (`fb:pulse`, #636) since the first
feedback builds. Problem notes become work issues. Praise and pulse answers come here instead, so we keep what works
and don't polish it away.

**Privacy (#637):** this repo is public. Each row puts the point in our words and links the private note. No tester
quotes, ids, devices or box names. Notes from before 2026-11-13 were filed in the public repo, so their links point
there.

**Keeping it current:** triage adds a row for every new `fb:praise` note and every `fb:pulse` answer, then labels the
note `fb:triaged` (see [feedback.md](../../feedback.md)). Pulse tables: `bash Tools/pulse-report.sh`.
