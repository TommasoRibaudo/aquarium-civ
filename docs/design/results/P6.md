# P6 Eras & Shelf: build status (2026-10-29)

Tests H6 ("an era transition witnessed live is a payoff worth waiting for; one of the two growth forms motivates
clearly more"). Spec: [prototype-plan.md](../prototype-plan.md) §5. Scene `Assets/Prototypes/P6_ErasAndShelf/P6_ErasAndShelf.unity`
(menu: AquariumCiv → Agent → Prototypes → Build P6 Eras and Shelf), opened from the Test Hub. Lab only. Results go here
after testing.

## Build plan
- **Hypothesis:** watching a new era arrive live is worth waiting for, and one growth form motivates clearly more.
- **Same in both:** a staged Camp → Hamlet → Village. Eras are plateaus: when the people are ready (Hamlet at 10 people,
  Village at 16) the world pauses and waits, and a big "Watch" button appears; a threshold crossed while away waits too
  ("Glasswick waited for you"). The live moment (~10 s): the people gather in the square, the buildings are rebuilt in
  the new style one by one (Camp = tents, Hamlet = wooden homes, Village = painted homes and a tower hall), then a cheer
  with confetti and a chronicle line.
- **The one variable:** what happens at Village, when the box is full.
  - **A · The box grows:** an annex box rises beside the tank and joins it; the camera pulls back; the village spreads
    into the annex (its tiles become building slots).
  - **B · New box on the shelf:** the camera pulls back to a shelf; a new box rises beside the old one with a fresh camp
    whose settlers carried the old banner (a relic). The old box stays alive as a museum (frozen in time, its people
    still walk). "Old box / Shelf / New box" buttons visit either.
- **In scope:** the three era looks (KayKit only), the live threshold moment, both growth forms as scripted sequences,
  a shelf view with two boxes.
- **Out of scope:** new systems per era, nudges, history views, a diary.
- **Simplest readings:** "the lid opens" is left out (the box already has an open top); the annex rises from below
  instead. The museum is a frozen copy of the old world.
- **Metrics:** `era` (ready with how: live, return or debug; watch with seconds waited; done with seconds in the
  transition), `growth` start/end with form and seconds, `shelf` focus changes with dwell, counters `transition_s`,
  `old_box_s`. Survey Q4 "I want to see what comes next".
- **Lab pacing (~7 min per variant):** a 12-year-old camp at ×60 → the Hamlet threshold in about 2 minutes (or step
  away +1 h) → watch → step away +24 h → "it waited for you" → watch the Village transition and the growth form →
  explore for a minute → Done.
- **Debug panel (P6 row):** Threshold (next era), Watch now, Skip to Village + grow, time jumps, Fresh lab world.

## Agent playtest
- **Run 1 failed** on six real findings, all fixed: A's annex never got a building (and the banner said the box was full
  when 14 tiles were empty); B's shelf chips sat on top of the hidden-but-live Step away row; the Hamlet took ~5 minutes
  at ×60; a museum person's card showed someone from the new box; `old_box_s` lost the last visit; the return stats
  line was swallowed by the threshold banner. Run 1 passed: era looks, the moment, 0 errors, the plateau, B's museum
  and new box, real-touch wipe/rotate after growth, a clean variant switch, logs, 31/31.
- Re-verify (main session, play mode): the Hamlet threshold within a minute; after +24 h the welcome line and "waited for
  you" together; the annex holds 5 buildings and people right after it rises; B's chips alone at the bottom; a museum
  person's card matches the name over their head.

## Polish for the user to decide (not fixed)
- The rebuild pops every building at once, and Camp → Hamlet changes only the huts; at default zoom the world change is
  small, so the banner and confetti carry the moment.
- A's annex is a second complete glass box (double wall at the seam, clean glass while the main glass is grimy; people
  walk through the walls).
- In the wide views people are specks; the relic flag is hard to see; the look sampler reads "empty" between boxes.
- After the shelf appears, the new box's grime shows on the museum's glass.
- A Game-view resize (iPad split view, rotation) drops the wide framing; a variant switch keeps the camera's yaw.

## Open questions
