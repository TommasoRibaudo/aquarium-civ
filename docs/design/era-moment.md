## Choices for the user

Recommended option first, marked ★.

1. **How the moment starts.** ★ **A "Watch" button** on the era card and in the thumb zone while the era waits; it
   never starts by itself · tap the waiting leader in the box (no button) · it plays automatically when you open the app.
2. **Hurry or skip.** ★ **Hold to hurry** (plays at 2×), no skip: the moment is the payoff and only happens twice per
   box · a Skip button · neither.
3. **How the rebuild looks.** ★ **A wave from the centre outward**: people walk to each lot and it rebuilds there with
   a puff, one after another · everything at once (P6; the change was too small to notice at phone size) · scaffolding
   that finishes over the next game years, after a short moment.
4. **When the notification question comes.** ★ **At the end of the first era moment**, as its last beat ("Want a note
   when the next one is ready?") · when you first see the waiting state, before watching.
5. **What the era festival does.** ★ **As research 09's Era Festival: bunting and a party, plus one game year of
   slightly higher mood** [est: +5 %] · a party only (story and view, no effect on the sim).
6. **When the town has reached its peak.** ★ **The box is full** (no good lot left for 3 game years) **and the town is
   at least 40 years old and has filled all 7 seats once**. Then it waits for you, like an era · a fixed age (e.g. 60
   town years) · you may end the box whenever you like once the town is 40 years old (Islanders' choice).
7. **Your last gesture in a box.** ★ **One long wipe across the old glass**, which polishes it into museum glass · tap
   the brass plate to engrave it · none (the last moment plays as a scene).
8. **Life in the museum.** ★ **Alive but steady**: people keep their day, age, marry, have children and die; nothing
   new is built; the glass doesn't get dirty; their stories wait for when you visit · frozen in a looping day (P6's
   frozen copy; cheapest).
9. **How the founders get to the new box.** ★ **They walk across a ruler** the Keeper lays from box to box (an object
   from the Keeper's room) · they're already inside when the new box arrives · the Keeper ferries them in a teacup.
10. **Who founds box 2.** ★ **The lord's younger child** (the one who won't inherit) leads, with two young notable
    families the game picks; you can swap the leader for your pinned notable · your pinned notable's family leads ·
    you pick all three families.
11. **Box 2's setting.** ★ **Desert oasis** (the furthest from box 1's green river valley) · mountain village · you
    choose between the two, with a hint of each (Islanders: New Shores).
12. **Which relics travel.** ★ **You pick 3 on the shelf** (research 09's Founding Bundle), with 3 already ticked as a
    suggestion · the game picks.

## 1. What other games do (research)

**[V]** primary source, **[S]** secondary (wiki, press, forum), **[est]** my reading.

| Game | How an era or a run ends | Lesson for us |
|---|---|---|
| **Civilization VI: Rise and Fall** | Big events are **Historic Moments** written on a **Timeline** "remembered by future generations"; when a new world era starts, your Era Score decides a Dark, normal or Golden Age [S] | A **timeline of moments** is a good shape for the book. Judging the player at the era change is not ours |
| **Civilization VII** | At an Age transition you pick a new civilization, spend Legacy Points, and **lose most units**; bonuses carry over instead [V][S]. Reviewers found the break jarring [S] | An era change that **takes things away** feels like a loss. Ours keeps every lot and every person: continuity is the point |
| **Frostpunk** | The final storm, then an ending that looks back over your laws and asks if it was worth it [S] | The end of a box should **look back over its whole history**. No verdict on the player |
| **Against the Storm** | Win a settlement and you can keep playing, but **nothing more happens** (no rewards, time stops mattering); leaving wipes it, meta upgrades stay [S] | A finished place has to be worth visiting **for its own sake** (its people and history), and leaving must never wipe it |
| **Islanders** | A gauge fills; **you click it when you want** to move to the next island, or stay and keep building [S]. In *New Shores* you **choose between two islands** with a hint of each [S] | The player decides when to leave. A choice of setting with a hint is cheap and pleasant |
| **Animal Crossing: New Horizons** | At a 3-star island rating, K.K. Slider's first concert **rolls the credits**; next, Tom Nook hands you the song and the concert comes back every Saturday; the game goes on [S] | A cozy finale is **a party, then life goes on**, with a keepsake |
| **Stardew Valley** | Grandpa's evaluation at the start of year 3: a quiet visit to his shrine, candles lit from your first two years [S] | A **ritual look-back** at a small shrine. We skip the score |
| **Foundation** | A harvest festival added "to break the rhythm of the villagers' daily toil" ([life-in-the-box](life-in-the-box.md) §1) [V] | A festival breaks the daily loop after the moment |
| **Our P6** | One tester, variant B 5/5/5 ("Yes yes yes!"). Watched 10.6 s and 15.7 s. The rebuild popped everything at once; at default zoom the change was small, so the banner and confetti carried it ([P6](results/P6.md)) | Make the change itself the show: **a wave, a guided camera, a longer moment** |
| **Our Growth Lab** | Tents → huts → half-timbered houses → townhouses **on the same lots**; eras told apart 9/9 in stills; the box is full by year 100–120 ([growth-lab](spikes/growth-lab.md)) | The rebuild in place reads. "Full" is a natural peak |

What we take: continuity over reset (Civ VII as the warning); a timeline of moments (Civ VI); look back at the end
(Frostpunk, Stardew); the player picks when (Islanders); a party, then life goes on (Animal Crossing); a finished
place worth visiting (Against the Storm as the warning). What we never take: scores and verdicts, losing things at
the change, timers.

## 2. The era-ready waiting state

The sim marks the era ready (`eraReady`, already built) when the thresholds are met: village at pop 10, 6 buildings,
10 years; town at pop 22, 13 buildings, 20 years in the village. The people keep living, live and offline. The era
waits, for days if need be. Nothing gets worse while it waits.

**What you see in the box:**

| Waiting for | The people are preparing | The leader |
|---|---|---|
| Village | Seed baskets stacked by the fire, a stake by every tent, a cleared strip of ground at the edge. The elders sit together more | The chief stands at the fire in the evening, looking up at the glass |
| Town | Timber and stone piled on the green, a market bell hung but not rung, scaffolding poles leaning on the huts | The headman paces the green, a roll of plans under an arm |

If it waits for long, the piles grow a little (a comic stack of seed baskets), never anything sad.

**What you see on screen:**
- **The chronicle line** (decided, built): "Glasswick is restless. The elders talk of staying put and sowing seed;
  something is about to change." It's the **last card** of the stack, after the good news, with a **Watch** button.
- **The Watch button** stays in the thumb zone, under the box, as long as the era waits: "Watch them become a village".
  Small, warm, no counter, no timer.
- **The glance** peek shows it: "Glasswick is ready to become a village · Watch".
- **The close**: the evening talk's teaser is replaced by "They'll wait for you" (first-session §4, option C).
- **Notification** (opt-in, decided): one local notification when an era becomes ready while you're away. Asked once,
  at the first era moment (Choice 4).

**Before it's ready:** at 80 % of the thresholds [est] the teaser hints at it ("The elders talk about planting seeds
and staying put"), as in first-session §4.

## 3. How the player starts the moment

Tap **Watch** (Choice 1). The UI slides away; only a small "hold to hurry" hint stays for the first 3 s (Choice 2).
If the glass is very grimy, a soft hint first: "Wipe for a clear view?" with the wipe ready, and "Watch anyway". It
never blocks.

**If you leave mid-moment**, nothing is lost: the sim moves the era only at the cheer beat. Before it, the era is
still waiting and the moment plays again from the start next time. After it, the festival is already running.

The sim clock holds still during the moment (no months pass). Mess doesn't grow. The camera is guided, but one finger
can still orbit; let go and it eases back to the guided path.

## 4. Camp → village (~60 s)

The first moment is shorter: fewer lots, and the player hasn't seen one before.

| Time | Beat | In the box | Camera, light, sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Dawn** | Whatever time it was, the light eases to early morning. Everyone stops and walks to the fire ring | Camera glides low over the fire, three-quarter view. The ambient fades; a wooden flute starts a slow tune |
| 0:08 | **The chief speaks** | The chief steps to the fire; everyone looks at them. Bubble: "We stay. This is home." They push a stake into the ground by the fire | Close on the chief. A soft drum once |
| 0:15 | **The green** | The fire ring becomes **the green**: grass spreads in a ring, the fire shrinks to a hearth, **a well rises in the centre** (research 08 §3.7) | Small pull back. The tune picks up |
| 0:20 | **The wave** | From the centre outward, people walk to each tent: a puff, and **a thatched hut stands on the same lot**. Gathering grounds become fenced strip fields (lines draw across them); the woodpile becomes a woodshed. Lots never move | The camera orbits ~60° with the wave and pulls back to the whole box. Each rebuild: a wooden knock, each one a note higher, so the wave plays a rising scale. A light haptic tick per lot |
| 0:40 | **The headman** | The chief takes a sash from an elder: **the chief becomes the headman** (same person, decided). Bubble: "Headman Ada, then." People laugh | Close on the pair |
| 0:46 | **The cheer** (the sim moves the era here) | Everyone turns to the glass, waves and cheers. Hats in the air, a few petals (not confetti) | Light swells to warm midday. A full chord. Caption: **"Glasswick · a farming village · year 14"** |
| 0:53 | **The new tool** | The shaping button glows; the **channel** slot opens with its hint: "Drag from the water" (research 10: the era's first tool comes with the moment) | A bright chime |
| 0:58 | **The festival begins** (§6) | Bunting goes up on the green | Camera free again |
| ~1:05 | **First time only: the notification question** (Choice 4) | "Want a note when the next one is ready?" Yes / Not now | — |

**What carries over:** every person, every lot, every path; the chief (now headman); jobs change where the era
needs it (hunter → shepherd, gatherer → farmer; `JobsOnEraChange`, built). The miller and priest seats open later,
when their buildings exist (notable-roles §2).

If the chief's seat is empty at that moment, the notables pick the first headman on the spot: a quick vote beat in
place of 0:40 ("The village chose Ivo").

## 5. Village → town (~90 s)

Bigger: more lots, taller buildings, a market square. It changes in kind (crafts and guilds, not industry).

| Time | Beat | In the box | Camera, light, sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **The bell** | Morning. The priest rings the new market bell for the first time; everyone comes to the green | Camera high over the green. The bell, then a fiddle tune over the village's flute |
| 0:10 | **The headman speaks** | On the well's step: "We've outgrown ourselves." People cheer | Close on the headman |
| 0:18 | **The square** | The busiest junction becomes the **market square**: cobbles spread out from the well, stalls appear round its rim (research 08 §3.7) | Pull back. Cobble taps |
| 0:28 | **What's cleared** | If anything is cleared (only here, never silently: research 08), it's shown: the old camp hearth is lifted out and set at the square's edge as a stone. Chronicle: "The old camp ring was cleared for a market. Someone kept the stone." | A pause in the music, one soft note |
| 0:35 | **The wave** | Outward from the square: huts on the main path become **half-timbered houses**, and the busiest become **two-storey townhouses**; scaffolding flicks up and falls away. The main path becomes a cobbled road, unrolling from the square. Fields merge into larger striped fields. Workshops go up at the water (a watermill if there's a channel) | Long orbit, ~120°, slowly pulling back. Each rebuild: a hammer knock rising up the scale |
| 1:05 | **The lord** | The headman is handed a cloak and a chair is carried to the market steps: **the headman becomes the first lord**, and the lordship will pass in their family (decided: inherited lord). Their eldest child stands behind the chair. Miller and priest keep their seats | Close on the steps |
| 1:15 | **The cheer** (the sim moves the era here) | Everyone faces the glass and cheers; banners from the windows | Light to bright midday. Full chord. Caption: **"Glasswick · a town of crafts and guilds · year 38"** |
| 1:22 | **The new tool** | The **outcrop** slot opens: "Press and hold on a hill" | Chime |
| 1:28 | **The festival begins** (§6) | A fair on the square | Camera free |

**What carries over:** every person, lot and path; headman → lord; miller and priest stay. The other town seats
(guildmaster, master builder, merchant, captain of the watch, innkeeper) open one by one as their buildings appear,
as now. **The lord appoints** the merchant and the captain of the watch (notable-roles had the mayor do it; the
inherited lord replaces the mayor).

The other two era tools come over the next ~10 game years (research 10): village hill & hollow and Keeper's Lamp;
town spring and dye meadow. Older tools level up at the moment (rain → storm later, grove → orchard), with a one-line
note on their tray icon.

## 6. The festival

Research 09's **Era Festival** (a slice power-up, decided): the party after an era arrives.

- **Live part (~20 s after the moment, then it keeps going):** bunting and long tables on the green (village) or the
  square (town); a ring dance; a singer; kids running; the leader's toast. It replaces the evening for the rest of
  the visit.
- **For one game year** (one real hour): bunting stays up and evenings are festival evenings. Under Choice 5, mood is
  slightly higher for that year [est: +5 %], with no dice, so it never shifts the economy's rolls.
- **Chronicle:** "Glasswick danced for three days to welcome the new village. Ivo fell in the well (he's fine)."
- Traits colour it as any festival (notable-roles §4): a singer as headman means songs every night.
- Never time-limited: the era waits for you, so the festival waits too.

**The book** (Gate 2: small and polished) gets **one chapter per era**: "The camp years (1–14)", "The village years
(14–38)", "The town years (38–…)". Each chapter opens with **a still of its era moment**, taken at the cheer, and
lists the era's leaders and its 3–5 major events. The **time-lapse** puts an era marker at each moment; tapping it
replays the moment (re-staged from the snapshots, not video). When the box completes, the book gets its last page
(§8) and its cover takes the brass plate's name.

## 8. The box completing

### When: the town's peak (Choice 6)
The sim marks **peak ready** when all hold:
- the box is full: no lot scores well enough to build for 3 game years (the Growth Lab's "coarse scan" point);
- the town is at least 40 years old [est];
- all 7 town seats have been held at least once.

Growth Lab puts "full" at year 100–120, i.e. about 1–2 weeks of play (game-definition §3). Peak ready then works
like an era: a chronicle line, the Watch button ("Watch Glasswick's last evening"), the notification. **The town keeps
living** while it waits, as long as you like (Islanders: you choose when to leave).

### The last moment (~2.5 min)

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **The last evening** | Dusk. Every living notable walks to the market steps; the crowd fills the square. The lord on the chair, the heir behind |
| 0:15 | **The look back** | The lord's speech, three bubbles drawn from the book: the founding, the biggest story of each era ("We remember the year of the Sky Bread"). The camera visits each place as it's named |
| 0:45 | **The legends' lanterns** | One lantern rises for each legend in the roster (the dead notables), with their name as it floats up and settles against the glass like a star |
| 1:10 | **The founders** | A small group gathers at the edge with bundles: the founders of box 2 (Choice 10) and the 3 relics (Choice 12). Families hug |
| 1:25 | **Out to the shelf** | The camera pulls back through the glass to the shelf. A new empty box is set down beside the old one, with the same soft thump as the first run |
| 1:35 | **The crossing** | A ruler is laid from box to box (Choice 9); the founders walk across it in a line, the leader first, relics carried on their backs. They look down at the room and keep walking. Nobody says what they saw |
| 1:55 | **Your last wipe** (Choice 7) | A ghost finger over the old box: "One last wipe". The wipe leaves the glass gleaming, museum-clean. A small lamp lights above it |
| 2:05 | **The plate** | The brass plate gets its second line, engraved letter by letter: "Glasswick · years 1–118 · camp, village, town" |
| 2:15 | **Roll call** | The names of the box's people scroll up past the shelf, legends first, like Animal Crossing's credits (~20 s, tap to hurry) |
| 2:35 | **The new box** | The camera moves to the new box: the founders unpack by a fire in the sand. First card of box 2 (§9) |

### The museum on the shelf
- The finished box stays on the shelf, lit, with its plate. **Relics** stand on small plinths in front, ordered by era
  (research 09, the Animal Crossing fossil hall).
- **Tap it to visit.** It looks and works like your box did: people keep their day, you can tap anyone for their card,
  browse its time-lapse and book.
- **Alive but steady** (Choice 8): people age, marry, have children and die; nothing new is built; no eras. Its glass
  never gets dirty: one box needing care is enough. Its stories collect quietly and show as a card stack only when you
  visit ("While you were away in the oasis…").
- **Shelf Neighbours** (decided: a small trade between boxes, later): once two boxes are finished, a little bird
  crosses between them now and then with a recipe, a song or a name, and both chronicles mention it. Stories and
  looks only. Not in the slice.

## 9. The second box arrives

- **Setting** (Choice 11): one of the decided settings (**desert oasis**, **mountain village**; decisions.md wins over
  game-definition §8's older list). Same style kit, a different layout and palette accents. The setting's tools
  replace the standard ones in their slots (decided): the oasis has palm spring, dune shift, desert rain.
- **Founders** (Choice 10): **three families, ~7 people, 3 notables** (the camp starts with 3, decided). They keep
  their names, traits and family links: the family tree runs across boxes. The leader is the new camp's first chief.
  The old box loses them, as a story ("The lord's youngest left with the Lund twins").
- **Relics** (Choice 12): ~3 travel (decided); the rest stay on the old box's plinths, never lost. Each gives its small
  start or story hook (research 09). In the slice: the carrying is shown, the effects are a stub.
- **First beats in box 2:** the founders unpack at a fire; the chief looks up and says something about the walk across
  the sky. The first card: the founders line (§7). Then the first-run rhythm again, minus naming (you name the new
  settlement on its plate the first time you open it) and minus "Their first year".
- **In the slice**, box 2 arrives and that's all (vertical-slice-plan §5): its own play comes later.

## 10. What the sim and the view need

**Sim** (`Assets/Game/Sim`, pure C#, lane A):
- **Era move at the cheer.** Keep `AdvanceEra()` as the only way in; the view calls it at the cheer beat, not at
  Watch, so a moment left half-way replays.
- **Roles at the change** (#27): in `AdvanceEra`, chief → headman, headman → lord (start the lord line: `lordLineId`,
  heir = eldest child); the lord appoints the merchant and the captain. An empty leader seat gets an instant vote.
- **Era festival:** `festivalUntilMonth` (one game year), a mood lift with no dice (Choice 5); a chronicle line.
- **Peak:** `peakReady`, `peakReadyMonth`, a `SimConfig` peak rule (years, seats, "no good lot for N years"), and
  `CompleteBox()` that writes the box-complete line and returns the founders and relics.
- **Prep state for the view:** `eraReady` already says enough; how long it waited (`eraReadyMonth`) sets the pile size.
- **Museum mode:** a cheap step for finished boxes (aging, births, deaths, marriages, stories; no building, no mess,
  no eras), run in catch-up only when you visit [est].
- **New box seed:** setting, the founder notables (copied with a link back to their old box and family), the relics.
- **Shelf save:** a `ShelfState` with the list of boxes (each its own `SimState`), the active box, relic plinths.
- **Notification time:** iOS can't run the sim in the background, so at close the app runs the sim ahead
  (deterministic, with the 8 h full / quarter speed rule) to find when the next era or the peak becomes ready, and
  schedules one local notification for then. Cancel it on open.
- **New chronicle kinds** (§7) and **book chapters per era** (the yearly snapshots already have `era`).

**View** (lane B):
- **A moment director**: the beats in §4, §5 and §8 as data (time, camera rail, light, audio cue, action), so timings
  can be tuned without code. Hold to hurry at 2×.
- **Rebuild in place**: per lot, the camp, village and town looks of each building type (Growth Lab has them), a
  puff, scaffolding, the wave order (by distance from the centre). Lots never move.
- **The green and the square**: fire ring → green with a well; green → cobbled square with stalls; cobbles spreading
  along the main path.
- **Waiting props** (seed baskets, stakes, timber and stone piles) driven by `eraReady`.
- **Festival** bunting, tables, ring dance (life-in-the-box's festival activity).
- **The still at the cheer** for the book (camera pose + year, re-rendered from the snapshot).
- **Shelf**: the camera through the glass to the shelf, the new box set down, the ruler and founders walking it, the
  museum lamp, the plate engraving, plinths, visiting a museum box. Per-box grime (only the active box).
- **Audio**: flute (camp), fiddle (village and town), the rising knocks, the bell, the chord. Never information in
  sound only: everything also shows (practices §10).
- **Debug panel** (CLAUDE.md): era ready now (built: `ForceEraReady`), play the moment, replay any past moment, hurry
  speed, peak ready now, complete the box now, start box 2 in each setting, visit the museum, set museum mode.

**Phone checks:** the moment's camera keeps people readable at portrait size; the wave runs at 30 fps with the
chunked rebuild (research 08 §3.8; the Growth Lab rebuilt the whole box at ~1 s in the Editor, too slow for a beat
per lot).
