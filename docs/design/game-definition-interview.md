## Batch 1
| Topic | Answer |
|---|---|
| Eras | "Hunt gather camp > Farming village > industrious town (not like industrial but you know, renaissance industry?)". Three eras: **hunter-gatherer camp → farming village → industrious (crafts/guilds, renaissance-style) town**. Whether a box "finishes" after the town: still open (batch 2) |
| Offline | "Everything slows down after 8 hours." Mess still caps at 48 h. How much it slows: open (batch 2) |
| Deep session (20 min) | All four: **shape the land more** (terraform, rivers, decorate), **history browsing** (time-lapse, small book, family trees, snapshots), **follow a person** (pin a notable and follow their day), **collections / relics** (found in the box, kept on the shelf, some carry over) |
| Business model | **Free first box + one unlock** (~$4–6 unlocks the shelf: more boxes, settings). No ads, no timers |
