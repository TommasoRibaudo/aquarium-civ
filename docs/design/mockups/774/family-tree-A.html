Family tree, take A: top-down generations

The whole family of one founding couple, top to bottom. Living faces in colour with a green dot, the dead greyed with a small stone. The open card's person has a brass ring. This sample is 33 people over 5 generations (1018 px wide, so it scrolls sideways and down; the corner map shows where you are). A branch can fold to a chip (“11 more”); tap a face to open that person's card.
