Family tree, take B: follow one line

One family, one line at a time. Pick a branch (Tilda's line) and the History page follows it down the generations like a page of the small book: a spine of years, the line person with a story, their siblings beside them. Each row is tappable; the “Whole family” chip opens the A-style tree. Scales by only ever showing one person per generation plus their siblings, so 5 generations and 40 people stay under about 6 screens.
