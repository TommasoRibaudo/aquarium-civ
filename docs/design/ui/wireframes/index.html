What to check

  1. Hold your phone one-handed. Open this page at full size and try to reach the bottom row.
  2. Turn the blur test on. The dark primary should be the first thing you see on every screen.
  3. Switch the world to Dirty and the phone to Small. Anything hard to read there is a problem now, not later.

Decisions drawn here

  1. History always opens the time-lapse. Unread stories live on the peek.
  2. Pin chip becomes a small portrait and name, apart from the tools.
  3. Box name and year are quiet text, with a small settings mark.
  4. Photo and Feedback become small icons. Feedback appears in test builds only.
  5. Year stays on, quietly. Era gets no sign in the HUD.
  6. Shape loses its strong look. The strongest look belongs to each moment's main thing.
