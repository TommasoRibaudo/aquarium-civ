# UI proposal: questions for the user (#107, 2026-10-31)

Each line has a recommendation. Answering "use the recommendations" accepts them all.

## Decisions

1. **Cancel while placing a tool:** A, bottom row: fixed, always in reach, far from the footprint. B, beside the footprint: short travel, but it moves and can catch a tap meant for the ground. ★ A.
2. **Backing behind the box name and top icons:** only once the camera leaves its home view, or always. ★ Only off the home view.
3. **Long cards:** scroll inside 30 %, or grow to about 34 %. ★ Scroll, snapped to whole lines.
4. **Story cards with no filled button:** ★ Keep; only Give, Watch or Remember get one.
5. **Shelf and Stories share one chip slot:** ★ Stories wins; Shelf moves into Settings while it's up.
6. **Sheets over 30 %:** picker 33 %, licences 35 %, settings and feedback 59 %, silent notice 63 %. ★ Accept, with the silent notice inside the Sound row so settings stays at 59 %.
7. **Next-box picker:** two settings, desert oasis preselected. ★ Yes, with a sub-line on Mountain village.
8. **New game text:** Stories, Back to the story, the silent notice, the reset body, The next box, Begin, Rename your box, About; samples Tob, The Guild Years, The Quarry family, the snapshot file name, licence rows; doc lines used as game text. ★ Approve, and change "How often" to "Music plays".
