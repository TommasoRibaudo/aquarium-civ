# The first session and the check-in loop (draft, 2026-10-29)

> **Decided by the user 2026-10-29** (decisions.md "First session", "Session close"): wipe the glass *and* clear an overgrown patch first; name the **settlement** (shown on the aquarium plate); one-time "Their first year" skip; Sky Crumb with the second shaping; **the evening talk closes a long session (~15 min), short check-ins end quietly**; notifications asked at the first era-ready; one-line peek for the glance. ## Choices for the user

Recommended option first, marked ★.

1. **First-run order.** ★ **Wipe first, then name the box** (the first touch is the gesture that "felt amazing" in
   P1, and you name something you've already seen) · name first, then wipe (the order in the slice plan).
2. **What you name.** ★ **The box** (a brass plate on the plinth, a suggested name already filled in, one tap to keep
   it), and **the people name their camp themselves** later in a chronicle card · you name the settlement · no naming.
3. **How the first rain gets its answer in the first session.** ★ **"Their first year": a one-time live time-skip**
   (~15 s, one game year, only in the first run) that ends on the first chronicle card · no skip: the answer is the
   top card of your first return (Neko Atsume's "they come while you're away"), and the first run has no card ·
   no skip, but the first rain is answered within one game month (~5 real minutes) [est].
4. **When the Sky Crumb falls in.** ★ **With your second shaping** (the grove, in an early check-in), so a return has
   its own surprise · with the very first rain (research 09's first idea; it crowds the first run).
5. **How a check-in ends (Q13).** ★ **The evening talk: a short tuck-in at the fire plus one true teaser line drawn
   from the sim** · the teaser line only (a caption, no scene) · the tuck-in only (no teaser) · the Keeper Doll as the
   closing beat. The era-ready state replaces the teaser when an era is waiting (all options).
6. **What starts the close.** ★ **Stepping back** (pinch out to the box on its shelf) **or about 20 s without a touch
   once the check-in beats are spent** [est] · a "Goodnight" button · only when you step back.
7. **When the game asks to send notifications.** ★ **The first time an era is ready**, with the reason on screen
   ("Tell me when an era is ready to arrive?") · in the first run · only from settings.
8. **What a glance shows.** ★ **A slim peek of the top card** (one line, doesn't cover the box; tap it for the full
   stack) · the full card stack on every open.

## 1. What other games do (research)

Markers as in research 09: **[V]** primary source, **[S]** secondary (wiki, press, guide), **[est]** my reading.

| Game | First session | The return | How a session ends | Lesson for us |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Neko Atsume** | Put out food and a toy, then the game wants you to **close the app**: cats only visit while it's closed [S] | Who came, what they left (fish, mementos) [S] | You leave on purpose, curious about who'll come | **Leaving is part of the game.** The first close should teach "they live while you're away" |
| **Animal Crossing: NH** | A guided arrival, a tent, a name for the island [est] | Each new day (5 am) opens with an island **news broadcast**; when nothing is newsworthy the host tells a **personal anecdote** [S] | Shops close, buildings finish "tomorrow": you leave expecting an overnight change [S] | **A ritual opener, and never "nothing happened"**: fall back on a small story. Avoid its absence penalties (weeds, cockroaches, villagers moving out) [S] |
| **Pokémon Sleep** | Set up the tracker, sleep | A fixed **morning reveal**: your sleep type, then the Pokémon gathered round Snorlax, researched one by one [S] | The reveal ends on who you befriended | **One sequence, same order every time**, good news first. Its sold incense and camp ticket break pillar 6 (09 §1.3) |
| **Duolingo** | A short lesson at once | The streak counter | A strong **celebration screen** (Duo spins and bursts into the streak flame) [V] | **End on a moment, not a menu.** Avoid the rest: loss-aversion streaks [V] and escalating guilt pings ("You made Duo sad") [S] |
| **Viridi** | A pot of succulents, a snail | Plants grow in real time; built to be tended **two or three times a week** [S] | Nothing to finish | A slow, kind cadence works. Its plants can die: we don't do that [S] |
| **Kind Words** | A room, lo-fi music, a letter to write | Replies to your letters | Fold a **paper airplane** with a kind note and let it go; **no counts** of anything [S] | A small warm act to close on, and no score to chase |
| **Townscaper** | No tutorial: click and it builds | — | You stop when the town looks nice; hidden builds reward curiosity (09 §1.1) [S] | **Teach by doing**; the player decides when it's done |
| **Tamagotchi** (anti) | Hatch an egg | It can't be paused; neglect kills it, a ghost over a tombstone. The designer wanted owners to feel responsibility that way [S] | Fear | Everything pillar 4 rules out |
| **Fallout Shelter** | A guided build of the first rooms [S] | Production and exploration run while closed; dwellers out in the wasteland can die while the app is shut [S]. You tap each room to collect [est] | When rooms are ready; you can "rush" at a risk [S] | **The return as chores and timers** is what we avoid; seeing your people again is what we keep |
| **Cats & Soup** | Cats cooking at stations | Log on, collect the gold, upgrade [S] | When the upgrades run out | The charm is **seeing the characters work**. Avoid its ad prompts for double earnings [S] |

What we take: a ritual opener (wave → cards, good news first, Animal Crossing and Pokémon Sleep); a closing moment
(Duolingo's shape, Kind Words' warmth); leaving taught as part of the game (Neko Atsume); teach by doing (Townscaper).
What we never take: streaks, guilt pings, timers to rush, ads on return, anything that dies or leaves while you're away.

## 2. The first run, minute by minute (~3.5 min [est])

No tutorial screens. Each hint shows once, at the moment it's needed, as a ghost finger with at most three words; it
comes back after 25 s idle, at most 3 times (P1's pattern). The camp is the slice's starting world: a fire, a few
tents, ~6 people, **2–3 notables, one of them the founder and first chief** (notable-roles §3).

| Time | Beat | What you see and do | What it teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **The box arrives** | The room, the shelf; a glass box is set down with a soft thump. The glass is misted over: a fire's glow and small shapes behind it. A ghost finger wipes: "Wipe the glass". If nothing happens for ~20 s, a child inside draws a smiley in the mist | The box is a thing on a shelf; the glass is yours |
| 0:10 | **The first wipe** | The mist comes off under the finger. The camp appears; the people look up, point and **wave**. When the glass is clear, the light floods in and they cheer ("Look at that sky!") | Care = clarity (§1). The wave is how every return starts |
| 0:40 | **You name it** | The blank brass plate on the plinth glows. Tap: one field with a suggested name already in it [est: from a word list], "Keep" or type your own | It's your box. (Choices 1 and 2) |
| 1:00 | **The first notables** | The chief stands at the fire with a marker over her head and says "Someone's watching over us." Hint: "Tap someone". Her **person card**: name, job and role ("Ada Lund, gatherer, chief of the camp"), trait, age, her brother, a friend, a rival. The other notables' markers light up | People have names, jobs, roles and relationships. The rival is a thread to follow |
| 1:40 | **The first wish** | A gatherer looks at the thin berry bushes and sighs; a wish bubble: "Mira hopes for rain." The **shaping button** appears in the thumb zone with rain glowing (rain unlocks after the first wipe, research 10 §4.3) | Notables wish; the Keeper gives |
| 1:50 | **The first shaping: rain** | Tap the button: a tray with one tool. Ghost finger: "Rub over a spot". A cloud gathers where you rub; it rains. People stop and look up, kids splash, the gatherers walk over and start picking. A small label: "The Keeper's rain" | One gesture per tool; the people react at once |
| 2:30 | **"Their first year"** (Choice 3) | One time only. A caption, "Their first year", and the box lives through a game year in ~15 s: the light sweeps through the seasons, people work, the wet ground becomes a **gathering ground** by the stream (the credited answer; the first rain's answer must land inside this year [est]) | Time passes in there; the people answer gifts |
| 2:50 | **The first chronicle card** | One card, slim, at the bottom: "After the Keeper's rain, Mira Lund found mushrooms by the stream. The camp ate well that winter." "Show me" flies the camera there | Cards are how stories reach you; fly-to |
| 3:10 | **Closing the app the first time** | The evening talk (§4): the people gather at the fire, the chief looks up at the glass. A teaser: "Mira and Tob keep sitting together at the fire." First time only, one caption: "They'll keep living while you're away. Come back whenever you like." The camera eases back to the box on its shelf | Leaving is fine and part of the game (Neko Atsume) |

Not in the first run: the notification ask (Choice 7), pinning, the time-lapse, the grove and herd, the Sky Crumb.

**The first few returns** each add one thing, by doing [est]:
- Return 1: three or more cards, the first real wipe of grime; the person card shows a **Pin** with a hint.
- An early check-in: the **grove** unlocks (research 10); planting it brings the **Sky Crumb** (Choice 4).
- When there's enough history (~day 2 [est]): a card's "Show me" offers the **time-lapse**.
- Once the chief leads a hunt-ready camp (year 3–6, research 10): the **herd**.

## 3. The glance and the check-in, beat by beat

Under ~15 minutes away (P2's threshold) there's no wave and no cards: the box is just there, alive, and you can
look, wipe or shape. Mess never gets a card; it shows on the glass only (pillar 5).

### The glance (≤ 60 s)
| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Open | 0–2 s | Catch-up is done before the first frame [est: ≤ 200 ms, slice plan §5.2]. The box is in view |
| **The wave** | 2–4 s | The people turn to the glass and wave (more of them the longer you were away [est]) |
| **Top card peek** | 4 s | One slim line at the bottom: the top story ("Ada and Bo had a daughter"). Tap → the full stack (Choice 8) |
| Maybe one wipe | — | The grime is there to wipe; nothing asks you to |
| **Teaser** | — | Under the peek, the teaser line (§4), so even a 20-second glance ends on a hook |

### The check-in (~3 min)
| Beat | Time [est] | What happens | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The wave** | 0:00–0:05 | As in the glance | Good news first starts here: the wave is happy, never worried |
| **Chronicle cards** | 0:05–1:15 | 3–7 cards, slim, at the bottom, **good news first**, then the rest by salience; pinned person +0.15. "Show me" flies to it; "Skip all" | Sad news (an old notable's death) comes after the good, warm, with a line for the legends roster. Unread cards wait for the next open, never lost |
| **Care** | 1:15–2:00 | Wipe the glass, toss 1–3 bits of junk, prune a vine. The cheer when spotless | Under a minute for a full clean (§5 rule 3). Never a card or a number about mess |
| **A shaping or a wish** | 2:00–2:45 | At most one wish card per return (research 10); its "Give" opens the tray with that tool chosen and the spot marked. Or open the tray yourself. The people react at once; the answer comes in 1–3 game years and is credited later | No timer on a wish; it just stops once given. A shaping can be taken back until it's used |
| **Optional** | any time | Tap a person, pin one, open the history | — |
| **The close** | 2:45–3:00 | The evening talk (§4) | Never blocks you: you can leave at any beat |

## 4. How a check-in ends (Q13)

The problem (P2 feedback #8, a tester after a night away): "there was not much to do ... it feels a bit like doing
nothing." The user's reading: that build had no families, chronicle, people doing things or shaping; the slice has
all four. Those fill the middle of a check-in. The end still needs a beat of its own, so the session closes on
something rather than trailing off.

**Rules for any close:** never says "nothing to do" or "come back in N hours"; no guilt, no timer, no counter; true to
the sim; short (≤ 8 s); skippable by just leaving.

| Option | What it is | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Tuck-in** | The people drift to the fire, lamps and windows light up, the camera eases back to the box on the shelf | Warm, a clear "done" moment (Duolingo's shape, Kind Words' tone). Same every time, so it becomes a ritual | Alone, it says nothing new. A game day is ~10 s at 1 h = 1 year, so this is a staged view beat, not the sim's night [est: depends on M3b's day cycle] |
| **B · Teaser** | One line about what's brewing, drawn from the sim: "Ivo has his eye on the chief's seat", "The gatherers are watching the wet ground", "Mira and Tob keep sitting together" | The curiosity hook the idle loop needs; always different; costs one caption | Alone, it's just text |
| **C · Era-ready state** | When an era is ready: the box shows the people preparing (bundles packed, a cleared field) and "They'll wait for you to watch" with the Watch button | Already decided (eras wait, P6). The strongest pull there is | Only a few times per box: not a general close |
| **D · Keeper Doll** | The doll (a slice relic) sits by the box on the shelf and faces you as you leave | A personal touch; shows how you play | It arrives late (10+ credited shapings), once per box; nothing for the first days |

**Recommendation: A + B, "the evening talk".** When the close starts (Choice 6), the people gather at the fire (or the
green, the market steps in later eras), the speaker is a role-holder (chief, headman, mayor), and their bubble is the
teaser. A few lamps light, the camera settles on the box on its shelf. When an era is ready, **C replaces the
teaser**, and the first time it also asks about notifications (Choice 7). Once found, **D** sits on the shelf in the
pulled-back view, as garnish, not the beat.

**Where the teaser comes from** (highest salience wins; never the same one twice in a row):

| Sim state | Example line |
|---|---|
| A gift not yet answered | "The gatherers keep checking the wet ground." |
| Two single adult notables who are friends | "Mira and Tob keep sitting together at the fire." |
| A seat coming free or a challenge due (notable-roles §3) | "Ivo has started speaking at the fire. The chief has noticed." |
| An open wish | "Ivo still dreams of deer on the east meadow." |
| An era nearly ready (≥ 80 % of the thresholds [est]) | "The elders talk about planting seeds and staying put." |
| A rivalry | "Bo and Hal aren't speaking again." |
| Nothing pending | A true slice of life from what people are doing now: "The kids are building a raft." (Animal Crossing's anecdote) |

A teased thread gets a salience boost, so how it turned out is a card on your next return, however late. Nothing
teased can be missed.

## 5. What pulls you back

- **Curiosity from the sim:** the teaser, an unanswered gift, a pinned person's life, a relic you haven't seen.
  Everything waits for you: cards stay until read, eras wait at the threshold, nothing is missed for being away.
- **The opt-in era notification only** (decided): one notification when an era is ready to arrive. Asked the first
  time an era is ready, with the reason on screen (Choice 7). No daily reminders, no "we miss you", no mess pings.
- **The home-screen widget, after the slice** (decided): the box small, closed and tilted. It could later show the
  latest teaser line [est].
- **Nothing that punishes absence:** mess caps at 48 h and only dims the light; offline slows to a quarter after 8 h
  without saying so as a loss; nobody dies or leaves from neglect; no streaks.
