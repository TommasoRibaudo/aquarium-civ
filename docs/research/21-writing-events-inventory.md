# Aquarium Civ: inventory of player-facing text (read-only research, branch p9/signoff)

Compiled from code only (no Editor, no edits). Everything quoted is verbatim from the C# source. There are no .asset/.uxml/JSON text tables for game text: the only `.asset` files under Assets/Game are fonts and a UI panel settings. Line numbers are approximate to the file as read.

## How to read this

- A "system" below is a surface the player sees text on. Items have: id, system, source, trigger, text, named?, choices, memory, seen?, frequency (dense bullets; a field that does not apply is omitted). Large tables of one-line variants (trait lines, bubbles, teasers) are grouped under one item.
- Reachability: the game ships with the **living land** (ecology) ON in builds (`GameClock.EcologyPref` default `OnInBuilds`; OFF in the Editor), dilemmas ON in builds (`p7.dilemmas` default 1), moments ON, small choices ON. On a living land: the **Shape button is hidden** (GameHud.cs:485: no shaping tray, so no Rain/Grove/Herd/Channel/Lamp), **new wishes are never raised** (`WishStep` returns when `EcologyOn`), and every chronicle line passes through `LandWords` (goat/hen/stream/hut swaps, SimCore.cs:493-494). Items that need shapings are marked "unreachable on a living land".
- **There is no expedition, equipment, decoration or "charm" system on this branch** (the P10 "house expeditions" work is not in code). The only matches are: the `term_expedition` office line (section 6.1), the "charms on the Keeper's tree" choice prop (section 1, camp_remember), the five relics (section 7) and the choice props (section 1).
- "named?" says whether a specific person/place/thing the player placed is inserted. Almost all chronicle text names notables (first/full names); weather/world lines are generic.

## Mechanical oddities noticed (short list; details in the sections)

1. **`small_story` ("Who tells the story at the fire tonight") changes nothing.** Both options only leave a feather prop and write a line. The real storyteller is chosen by `LifePlanner.Day.cs:191-206` (winter eldest of age >= 60, else an elder by turns) and never reads the Keeper's pick. The hints ("tells it slow, with all the voices") are never staged.
2. **`custom` flags are write-only.** `winter:*`, `remember:*`, `harvest:*`, `spring:*`, `travellers:*`, `pond:*` are pushed to `State.customs`, but no callback sets `needsCustom` and `HasCustom` has no other reader. So `village_harvest` (feast/offering) and `camp_remember` (stone/tree) have NO sim consequence beyond a prop and 2 callback lines each; their memory is the card/book/Keeper's choices page only.
3. **The dilemma file's own promise ("never a better economy") is untrue for 4 of 8 defs:** options with a `lean` give x1.35 food or x1.5 wood production for 36 game months (camp_winter x2, camp_travellers "full packs", village_spring x2, village_hill_family "across the pond"), an invisible economy nudge.
4. **Living-land word swap misfires:** `LandWords` replaces any "a goat" and "the woodpile": the village_hill_family done line becomes "They moved in with the warmest spot in the tent."; the camp_winter woodpile answer becomes "The Keeper chose the camp. The axes rang till the first snow." while no wild wood has given wood. Goats survive in term/role lines ("a joke about the goats", "telling the goats it was rigged", "blessing on the goats"), in "Not you, goat", the tavern sheep, and in the choice props ("a little goat pen", "the pond family's goat").
5. **Same joke, three surfaces.** The four town-only trait stories equal bank lines 35-38 (card text) and `BankFirstPerson` (bubble), and their camp/village stand-ins are word-for-word bank lines 11-12. One event can be told as a card, a narrator bubble and a first-person bubble.
6. **Text about things not in the tank:** hens/fox/kitten (swapped), goats (partly), a sheep in the tavern, "Hat Day", "sack race", "egg-and-spoon", "pies", "chimney", "library", "a very large dog" (captain), "the Keeper's lamp" and "whiskers/cat" in stage 3 even if the Lamp was never placed, "far edge, where the world turns to glass" and "tower tall enough to see over the glass" said by the narrator in the camp/village (the "no glass before the town" rule is enforced only for bubbles, not chronicle text), "Pip" (a fixed kid's name in bank lines 3 and 27), "hills" (lost story), "sock/crops/garden" (dead mood lines).
7. **Dead / unreachable text:** `CareConfig.MoodLines` and `CheerLines` (10 lines, no reader); the `duck_pen` choice and `duck_pen_fence` line (nothing sets `duckPen` outside a debug cheat); Sky Crumb, Lens, tap-back, gift teasers, nudge-answer lines, wish cards, rain/lamp quotes, strange-light "Lamp" lines (all need a shaping, whose button is hidden on a living land); bank lines 13, 19, 20, 32 and most `moment`-keyed quotes (only `evening` and `any` are requested by the bubble code); `ducks` story (old boxes only).
8. **"Let them decide" disagrees with itself for the white stag:** the card path uses the chief's trait (`LetBeTraits`), `WhiteStagLetThemDecide()` uses a seed hash 50/50.
9. **Misnamed/odd ids:** callback `hill_neighbours_eggs` is about fish; `spring_timber_squirrels` is about roofers; `camp_travellers` "full packs" ends with the same two people joining later (`joins = true`), so both options give +2 people; `village_hill_family` "across the pond" has no `joins` (they stay apart for good).
10. **Teasers that never pay off:** the 16 "slice of life" evening lines ("The kids are building a raft.") never resolve (the thread just dies when the next one is told); the rivalry teaser says "aren't speaking again" even the first time; the office teasers are "paid off" by any later `term_*` line, which is text only.
11. **A callback always starts with a credit:** "Because the Keeper chose X, ..." (or "Because the people chose X, ...") even where the body ("the tavern keeps one long table...") reads as the people's own custom; for a lapse the credit goes to the people.
12. **`MakeStrangers` always partners the first two strangers**, so a "family of three" (village_hill_family) is a couple plus one more adult (age 18-35), not a child.
13. **The new-leader moment bubble says "Long live the new chief!"** for a headman or a lord too (PeopleVoice.Moments.cs).
14. **Era-moment "tool" lines ("Drag from the water", "Press and hold on a hill")** announce shaping tools; they show when `ShapingUnlocked(param)` for the era, but the Shape button is hidden on a living land, so the line may point at a tool the player cannot open (not traced further).

## Notation used below

- `{a}` `{b}` = first names of the two candidate notables (dead or alive at tell time); `{place}` = settlement name, or "the camp"/"the village"/"the town" before it is named (named at pop 12); `{land}` / `{camp}` = "the woods" / "the meadow" / "the pond" style words fixed when raised (#578); `{dead}` = name of the latest death (fixed when raised); `{tree}` = charm-tree words; `{p}`/`{q}` = the option's person / the other one (trait hints only); `{n}`, `{n1}`, `{n2}`, `{child}` = small-choice words.
- "named?" = does the text insert a specific name. "seen?" = is there something in the tank that goes with it.
- Texts quoted verbatim from code. Sentence-initial lowercase is capitalised at render (`DilemmaCap`).
- Living-land swap: on a living land (`EcologyOn`, default ON in builds, OFF in the Editor) every chronicle line passes through `SimCore.LandWords` (SimCore.LandWords.cs) which replaces some goat/hen/stream/hut words. Which entries it touches is noted where known.

### 1. Big dilemmas (Keeper's choices, "the people ask the Keeper") — 8 defs, DilemmaDefs.cs

**Common mechanics (apply to all 8, and to small choices)** — SimCore.Dilemmas.cs
- Gating: `Config.dilemmas` must be on (builds: ON by default via PlayerPrefs `p7.dilemmas` default 1; Editor: OFF unless switched on). Never during the first run (`firstRun.active` clears the schedule). Only raised while the Keeper watches or on an app open (`BeginVisit`); never during offline catch-up.
- Pacing: first big one is scheduled 6-10 game years (= 6-10 real hours at 1 h = 1 year) after the clock starts; each later big one 6-10 years after the previous raise. A 3 real-hour gap (`DilemmaGapHours`) must have passed since the last raise of any choice. Also needs: no open wish, and 8 game months since the last wish ended. One open at a time. Never repeats a def in a box ("asked already in this box").
- Closing: never by the clock. Closes on (a) the Keeper's answer (`AnswerDilemma`), (b) "Let them decide" (`LetThemDecide`, credited to the Keeper), (c) shown on 3 visits (an open counts as a new visit after 15 min away) unanswered -> `LapseDilemma` (people take the default option, line "The people chose {label} themselves." kind `dilemma_result`, NOT credited), (d) a person the options point at dies (`CastGone` -> lapses as `Gone`).
- Default option when the Keeper says nothing / lets them decide (`DilemmaDefaultOption`): score per option = +2 if the role-holder's (or the asker's, when the def has no role) trait is in the option's `favouredBy`, + `Affinity(holder, person)` for person options, -100 for a dead candidate; ties -> seeded hash.
- Effects of ANY answered/lapsed option (`ApplyDilemmaEffects`): `takesRole` (person becomes the era's leader, old holder vacates, flag `chosenByKeeper` if answered, line `role_won` "{FullName} became {role} of the {era}."), `lean` (adds a `DilemmaLean` for 36 game months: Food = x1.35 food production, Craft = x1.5 wood production; `NudgeFood`/`NudgeWood` in SimCore.Nudges.cs), `ties = RivalOrGrace` (see 1.1), `custom` string pushed to `State.customs` (see oddity: never read), `strangers` (a family joins or lives apart).
- Always (`CloseAnswered`/`LapseDilemma`): chronicle line (the "done" text, kind `keeper`, salience 0.8, `e.keeper = true`; a lapse writes kind `dilemma_result` salience 0.55), `ScheduleCallbacks` (each option has 2; due 4-15 years after the end by a seeded hash; one fires per month at most), `LeaveConsequence` (a physical prop in the box within 1 game year).
- Keeper's choices page / History / book "The Keeper's choices" page list every choice with: ask (shortAsk), "The Keeper chose X." / "The Keeper let the people decide: X." / "The people chose X.", "It touched A, B and C.", and "It left <prop words> in the box." / "It will leave <prop words> in the box." (KeeperChoices.cs).
- Shown as: a dilemma card on return (CardKind.Dilemma, headline = question clipped to ~50 chars; buttons = the two labels + "Let them decide", note "Touch one."), and staged in the box by `ChoiceSceneView` (the two people/places get halos; tapping one answers = `DilemmaPath.Box`). After answering, a prop appears at the spot (ConsequenceProps view) and the callback comes back later as a story card/held moment (`CallbackMoment` pulses the prop).
- Credit prefix for every callback: "Because the Keeper chose {label}, " (answered / let-them-decide) or "Because the people chose {label}, " (lapsed) followed by the callback text with its first letter lower-cased. The card headline is that credit.
- Hints (option sub-lines) are `DilemmaCap(...)`'d; for TwoCandidates the hint is replaced by a trait-specific line (`traitHints`) when the person has one of the 14 traits.

#### 1.1 UnchosenTies (applies to camp_fire and village_headman only)
- If the unchosen is a "proud" trait (stubborn, grump, loud, brave, clever, tinkerer, worrier) AND both are free of rival/friend/partner AND hash roll < 0.6: they become **rivals**: chronicle `rivalry`: "{other.FullName} could not forgive being passed over for {chosen.FullName}. They have been rivals since." (sets rivalId on both; later feeds the teaser "X and Y aren't speaking again.", reconcile stories, role contests).
- Else if both have no friend/rival/partner link: they become **friends**: `friendship`: "{other.FullName} took it well, and wished {chosen.FullName} luck. They became fast friends over it." (sets friendId; feeds weddings/teaser "keep sitting together").
- Else `dilemma_grace`: proud -> "{other.name} sulked for a season, then let it go." ; not proud -> "{other.name} took it gracefully and wished {chosen.name} well."

### 4. Chronicle story events: the monthly story stream — SimCore.Story.cs (`StoryStep`), SimCore.cs

**Common mechanics.** Every game month (12 per real hour) `StoryStep` rolls `storyChancePerMonth = 0.07`; if it hits, ONE option is picked by weight from whatever is eligible now. All lines are chronicle entries (shown as return cards, History, person cards, speech-bubble sources, book), never a popup or choice. A line's wording rotates by how many of that kind were already told (`Told`), so repeats cycle through the variants. `Record()` adds a role-holder's coda (`TermCoda`, section 6.4) and passes the text through `LandWords` (living-land swap, see top). Dice come from `storyRng` only. Nothing here is a choice; the only sim side effects are the listed mood/food changes and the friend/rival/partner/mentor links.
Era words: {era} = camp/village/town (tribe before the camp gate); a building {1} is `Label(b)`: camp fire / hut(camp)/cottage(village)/house(town) / gathering grounds(camp)/farm / woodpile(camp)/lumber mill / chapel / shop / else the lower-cased type (well, windmill, market, tavern, workshop, guildhall, bakery). {3} = "the Bright One" in the camp, else "the Keeper".

### 5. Offices and roles (chronicle lines) — SimCore.Roles.cs

**Common:** seats exist per era (`RoleTable`): Camp: chief (contest; seat the camp fire). Village: headman (election, seat well/camp), miller (succession; needs windmill), priest (naming; needs church). Town: lord (inheritance), priest, innkeeper (succession; tavern), guildmaster (guild election; guildhall or tavern), master builder (work contest; lumbermill), merchant (appointment; market), captain of the watch (appointment; market). Checked each month; fills/terms/challenges mostly in spring (`CalendarMonth == SpringSeason`). The chief steps down at age 65 (unless the stubborn). Each line is `RoleLine(kind, text, salience, a, b, seat)`; kinds `role_*` are shown as cards ("role" group); "role_won"/succession lines for the leader queue a NewLeader held moment. Named: always (FullName). Seen: the Offices sheet, person cards, held moment (new leader speech), seat building in the box.
