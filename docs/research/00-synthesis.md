# Research Synthesis (2026-10-28)

Cross-cutting conclusions from 01-technology, 02-idle-design and 03-aquarium-references.

## Where the three reports agree
1. **The niche is open.** No shipped game combines a 3D glass diorama, an autonomous civ, offline idle growth and a care loop (03). The nearest are ANT (PC ant farm, Feb 2027), WorldBox (watching, no care) and the mobile idle-civ games (numbers and menus, nothing to watch).
2. **The care loop is both the hook and the biggest risk.** Withering mechanics read as forced check-ins (02, FarmVille), and harsh death drives players away (03, Tamagotchi). The rule both reports reach on their own:
   - neglect causes visible, reversible mess with capped penalties
   - nothing permanent is lost without warning
   - recovery is fast and satisfying
   - the citizens learn to do basic upkeep themselves as they advance
3. **The return moment is the core loop.** Phones won't keep the sim running in the background (01), and mobile idle sessions are short and frequent (02). So each session opens on a "while you were away" chronicle generated by the catch-up simulation, with named characters (03).
4. **The glass is the interface.** Grime, fog and condensation on the box show civ health without any UI (03). In URP that's a cheap rim highlight, a reflection and a dirt mask (01).
5. **The progression arc is a series of eras.** Each era is an act with new systems, and the diorama visibly changes scale (02, Paperclips; 03, expanding beyond the box).
6. **Watch as much as tend.** The player progresses from tending to planning, through standing orders and policies that run while away (02). Watching is rewarded live: tap a person to follow them, plus a bonus for observing (03).

## Technology baseline (pending confirmation)
Unity 6.3 LTS:
- **Rendering:** URP Forward+.
- **Simulation:** Jobs + Burst with GPU instancing. Entities (ECS) only if profiling demands it.
- **Saves and time:** Unity Cloud Save with Apple/Google sign-in for saves and server time.
- **Reminders:** Mobile Notifications (inexact scheduling, opt-in).
- **Offline catch-up:** an aggregate simulation, with offline credit capped.

## Monetization signals
Accepted: premium or free-to-try with one unlock, optional rewarded ads for small boosts, cosmetics/supporter packs.
Resented: paying to skip waits, gacha, limited-time events, guilt notifications.
