Credits for the published game: see `CREDITS.md` at the repo root.
Sound effects (#96): sources downloaded 2026-10-30, licenses checked on each page / in each pack's License.txt; only the
clips the cue library (`Assets/Game/Audio/Core/CueLibrary.cs`) uses are in the repo.
No CC0 fly buzz was found on Kenney or OpenGameArt (2026-10-30), so the mess layer's flies are made in code
(`CareSound.MakeFlies`), like the cheer, the wipe rub and the swish: no third-party asset.

#280 phase 1: recorded candidates for the opening (barren wind and sand hiss, frogs, soil trickle and drips, mister spray, loose stone, first water) are in `Ambience/` and `Sfx/Eco/`. Phase 2 (2026-11-04) wired them in and removed the code-made `barren_dry_loop.ogg` and `frogs_pond_loop.ogg`; the mister plays the recorded `mist_spray_loop` (the code-made `MistSound.Generate` stays as the fallback and a debug A/B). No CC0 recording of drying dirt ticking/crackling was found (BigSoundBank, OpenGameArt searched 2026-11-03), so there is none.

The mister's spray (#266, "fsss") is made in code, ours: `MistSound.Generate` filters white noise to a 2-7 kHz band with a slow swell and cross-fades the loop seam (2.4 s, 24 kHz mono). No third-party asset. The droplets, wet soil and puddles (`Shaders/EcoGround.shader`) are code and vertex colours too.

The chatter babble (#144) is made in code, ours: `ChatterBabble` synthesizes 4 voices x 6 clips at start-up, 22.05 kHz mono. No third-party asset.
The first-water sounds (#279) are two CC0 one-shots, the same files as #280's branch (`origin/vs/280-opening-sound`; whichever merges first logs the table rows, this is the second record): `Sfx/Eco/first_water_drop.ogg` from "Drops of water #2" (#1385), Joseph Sardin, BigSoundBank, https://bigsoundbank.com/detail-1385-drops-of-water-2.html, CC0 1.0 (one drop cut out, 0.9 s); `Sfx/Eco/first_water_bubble.ogg` from "Bubbles" in "40 CC0 water / splash / slime SFX", rubberduck, https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx, CC0 1.0 (`bubble_01`, trimmed, faded, mono). Added 2026-11-04.

#315/#316 (2026-11-04): the frogs and the voices are code-made, no new third-party file. The pond's frogs are single soft croaks
(`FrogCroaker.Generate`, 4 takes) instead of the recorded chorus: `Ambience/frogs_pond_rec_loop.ogg` (BigSoundBank "Frogs #2", CC0, see above)
stays in the repo but the game no longer plays it (the frog layer's level only drives how often a croak comes). The voices' new kinds
(laugh, call, shout, hum, giggle, chat; clips 6 to 11 of `ChatterBabble`) and their muffled takes (`ChatterBabble.Muffle`) are made in code like
the old babble. No CC0 voice or frog sample was searched for or downloaded in this pass (the Editor-free worktree run); recorded voices or a recorded soft croak stay an option once the code-made ones are heard on a phone.

#424 (2026-11-08): no new third-party file. The wolves' first howl (`wolf_howl`, `proc:howl`) is a placeholder made in code
(`CareSound.MakeHowl`: a breathy rising tone with a waver and a second voice a fifth under it, ~2.8 s). No CC0 howl was searched for in
this pass; a recorded one (Freesound/BigSoundBank CC0) can replace it by pointing the cue at a file. The arrivals' other sounds reuse
existing cues (`pluck` for the bushes pushing up, `first_water_drop` for the ducks and the fish bag).
